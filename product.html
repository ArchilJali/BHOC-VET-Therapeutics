BHOC

Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier

Product identity

Beyond the red blood cell system and its limitations.

BHOC means Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier. It is a cell-free hemoglobin oxygen-delivery platform designed for immediate, controlled microvascular and tissue-level oxygenation when red-cell-dependent oxygen delivery is insufficient, delayed or operationally constrained, while endogenous erythropoiesis recovers.

The intended product profile is donor-independent, designed to be red-cell-antigen-free and compatible across blood types, with long shelf life and practical room-temperature logistics. By moving oxygen delivery beyond dependence on intact donor red cells, BHOC is designed to address limitations created by blood-group matching, donor availability, storage, transport, expiry and access.

Across species, blood-group systems may be known, partially characterized or unknown. BHOC uses one core cell-free oxygen-carrier architecture intended to avoid red-cell blood-group antigens, while species-specific formulation, dosing, safety and efficacy remain independent evidence requirements. Administration, delivery speed and treatment volume remain species-specific and require independent evidence.

BHOC is not intended to reproduce every biological function of whole blood. Its core objective is more precise: preserve or restore effective oxygen delivery to tissues and cells when the red-cell system cannot provide it adequately or quickly enough.

Development profile

The intended product profile.

Immediate oxygen delivery

Support microvascular and tissue-level oxygen delivery when anemia, blood loss or red-cell limitations reduce effective oxygen transport.

Beyond blood-group barriers

A donor-independent, red-cell-antigen-free development profile designed to avoid dependence on blood typing, donor matching and crossmatching.

Room-temperature logistics

Long shelf life, practical storage and reduced dependence on donor-blood logistics are core product-development objectives.

Cell-free architecture

One core Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier architecture designed for controlled oxygen delivery across blood-group systems, with species-specific evidence still required.

Development boundary

Product objectives are not regulatory approval.

BHOC development requires product-specific and species-specific evidence for formulation, dosing, safety and efficacy. Historical HBOC and Oxyglobin evidence, regulatory records and veterinary real-world cases are maintained in Vet Real-World Evidence & Cases.

Our foundation is comparative biology: different species have different blood group systems, yet most vertebrates share hemoglobin-based oxygen transport. We explore how this foundation can inform species-specific research and development.
