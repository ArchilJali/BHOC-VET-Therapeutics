“We be of one blood, ye and I.”

The memorable line from Rudyard Kipling's The Jungle Book expresses something central to the BHOC Species & Biodiversity Protection Initiative: different species, the same biological need for oxygen.

One oxygen. Many lives. A shared responsibility.

From household companions to wildlife in remote habitats, oxygen delivery is essential to life. Our work in BHOC Veterinary and the Species & Biodiversity Protection Initiative investigates how Precision Oxygen Therapeutics and veterinary science may, with appropriate species-specific research, contribute to animal health and conservation medicine.

This work is still subject to scientific and regulatory evaluation; it is not a claim that BHOC is an approved treatment for endangered species. But the underlying idea is clear: protecting a species also means caring for the lives of individual animals.

We appreciate WWF's accessible way of encouraging people to care about wildlife. Choose a card, add a personal message and share it with someone you love.
