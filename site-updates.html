13 October 2026 · Shared scientific evidence

Five oxygenation and microcirculation research publications added

Relevant shared research: five Dyson/Singer studies were added to the BHOC Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation catalogue, providing background on tissue oxygenation, shock and organ perfusion. The studies are not veterinary BHOC efficacy data. The Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation catalogue now lists 215 records; the five BHOC Evidence Library collections contain 923 placements in total.
